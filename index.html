Privacy Policy

HabitFlow - Habits, Todos, Notes & Journal

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

1. Introduction

The HabitFlow mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

Key Point: All of your habits, tasks, notes, and journal entries are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). Your data is never uploaded to our servers — we don't even have servers. Your journal is encrypted and unreadable without your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

HabitFlow is designed to work without requiring any personal information from you. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

The content you create — habits, tasks, subtasks, notes, checklists, and journal entries — is stored exclusively in an encrypted database on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we may collect the following types of information:

Local App Data: Your habits, tasks, notes, journal entries, streaks, theme preferences, and onboarding status are stored locally on your device. Your entire database is encrypted with SQLCipher and the encryption key is held in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2.3 Device Information & Advertising Data

We may collect non-personal information about your device through our advertising provider, including:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Device Advertising ID (for ad personalization)
  • App version and usage statistics (ad impressions, clicks)
  • Approximate location (IP-based, for regional ad targeting)

3. Data We Do Not Collect

HabitFlow explicitly does not collect or process the following:

  • Your habits, tasks, notes, or journal entries: All of your content is stored locally in an encrypted database on your device. We never access, upload, or store it.
  • App interactions / Analytics: We do not track which features you use, how often you use them, or your in-app behavior.
  • Crash logs / Diagnostics: We do not collect crash reports or performance diagnostics.
  • Personal identifiers: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • Location data: We do not track your GPS location. AdMob may use approximate location at the country/region level.
  • Contacts or messages: We do not access your contact list, SMS, or call history.
  • Browsing history: We do not track your web browsing activity.
  • Development tools: No development or debugging tools are active in production builds of the App.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality (all data stays local and encrypted)
  • Save your preferences (theme, onboarding status) locally on your device
  • Deliver the reminders and notifications you schedule
  • Display relevant advertisements to support the App's ongoing development

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Local, Encrypted Storage

All of your data is stored locally on your device in an SQLCipher-encrypted database. SQLCipher provides transparent 256-bit AES encryption of the entire database file, so your habits, tasks, notes, and — especially — your private journal cannot be read by other apps or by anyone with physical access to your device. The encryption key is generated on your device and stored in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore). We do not operate any backend servers.

6.2 Backups

Backups are created only when you explicitly choose Export Backup. The backup is a JSON file saved to your device and shared only through the Android share sheet to the destination you pick (e.g., saving to Files or sending to yourself). Backup files are not encrypted — treat them like you would any exported personal document. Import Backup restores a file you select.

6.3 Reminders

Reminders you schedule are delivered by Android's local notification system on your device. Notification content (e.g., "Time for: Morning Run") is processed locally by the operating system and is not transmitted to us.

6.4 Ad Data

Advertising-related data (Advertising ID, approximate location, device info) is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google's AdMob servers. We do not retain or store this data ourselves.

6.5 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information, including full-database encryption and hardware-backed key storage. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Permissions We Request

HabitFlow requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet: Required for loading advertisements.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to show the habit and task reminders you schedule.
  • Schedule Exact Alarms: Required so your reminders arrive at the exact time you set.
  • Receive Boot Completed: Required to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Vibrate: Optional haptic feedback when you check in a habit.

Important: All permissions are used solely for the App's core functionality. We do not request background location, contacts, SMS, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive permissions.

8. Notifications

HabitFlow sends local notifications only:

  • Habit reminders at the time you set for each habit
  • Task reminders on the due date and time you set
  • A daily summary nudge in the evening (which is cancelled automatically for the day once you complete all your habits)

On Android 13 and above, notifications require your explicit permission, which the App requests on first launch. You can decline or revoke it at any time in Settings → Notifications or system settings.
